Exploring User Intent

I am currently focusing on understanding the core of what the user desires. My immediate task is to precisely define the parameters of their request to ensure accurate and relevant output.

Com base nos documentos fornecidos, vou criar esboços de interfaces (wireframes) para as telas principais do Sistema de Gerenciamento de Orientações Acadêmicas (SGOA).
